We've intercepted a message encrypted using a hybrid quantum-classical algorithm. 
The encryption key was generated by a quantum circuit implementing a variant of 
Simon's algorithm. However, our quantum engineers noticed something strange - 
the circuit seems to have a deliberate flaw that creates interference patterns.

Your mission:
1. Analyze the quantum circuit (quantum_circuit.qasm)
2. Find the period hidden in the quantum interference patterns
3. Use this period to derive the encryption key
4. Decrypt the message (encrypted_quantum.bin)

The quantum circuit appears to implement Simon's algorithm but with added 
rotations that create unique interference patterns. These patterns reveal 
the secret period needed for decryption.

Note: You'll need quantum simulation tools and cryptographic knowledge.
